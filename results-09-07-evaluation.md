The publisher is given a fixed number of publishes and a deadline while one subscriber never receives. `completed` means every publish returned; `publisher_blocked` means the publisher was still blocked at the deadline, which is the specified behaviour of a lossless lock-step design and a defect in one that claims not to block.

| Family | Outcome | Implementations | Publishes completed by the deadline: least / median / most |
|---|---|---|---|
| broadcast | completed | 47 | 1,000 / 1,000 / 1,000 |
| broadcast | publisher_blocked | 47 | 0 / 1 / 81 |
| payload | completed | 13 | 1,000 / 1,000 / 1,000 |
| payload | publisher_blocked | 6 | 0 / 40 / 64 |

A blocked publisher is not always an idle one. After these batteries the measuring process was still using at least half a CPU with nothing left to do: the publisher waits for the subscriber that never reads by spinning, and goes on using a CPU until the process ends. Each battery runs in a process of its own, so this costs the other measurements nothing; in a program it would.

| Family | Implementation | Batteries that left it | CPUs in use afterwards | CPUs in use while the stalled-consumer probe's publisher was blocked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| broadcast | EpochCaster (13-OpenRouter) | broadcast.stalled_consumer, broadcast.trace_stalled_consumer | 1.05 | 1.04 |
| payload | EpochCaster13Crouton_r64 | none | under 0.5 | 1.04 |

The stall-resilience dimension of the scores takes this into account: a publisher that the stalled-consumer probe left blocked and that used at least 0.5 of a CPU while blocked ranks below every publisher in its group that parked or completed, however many publishes it had completed. The rule uses the probe's own figure, which the records have from contract 1.5.0; a run before that is scored without it.

## What this run cannot show

- It is one run on one machine with 2 logical CPUs at GOMAXPROCS 2. Nothing here describes behaviour with more parallelism, on another processor, or on another architecture.
- Profile `standard`, seed 1. Scores from different profiles, different record contracts or different families are not comparable.
- A score ranks implementations against each other. It does not say that any of them is correct: correctness is what the gates above and the tests address.
- The batteries measure the workloads they define (see `measurement.md`). A workload that is not among them was not measured.
